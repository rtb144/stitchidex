# Installation & Portable .zip Guide

## Portable .zip (no installer)
- Download `Stitchidex-win-x64-portable.zip` from the Releases page.
- Right-click the `.zip` and choose **Extract All…**. Do not run the app from inside the zip.
- Open the extracted folder and double-click `stichidexdotnet.exe`.
- If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** then **Run anyway**.
- The `help` folder next to the app contains this whole wiki as plain text, so help works with no internet.

## Where your catalog is stored
Your catalog (tags, notes, previews) is saved in `%APPDATA%\Stitchidex\stichidexdotnet_catalog.sqlite3`. Deleting the app folder does not delete your catalog.

## Updating
Extract the new `.zip` over the old folder (or into a new folder). Your catalog is kept.
