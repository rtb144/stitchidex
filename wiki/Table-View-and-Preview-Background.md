# Table View & Preview Background

## Table View
Click **☰ Table View** in the top bar to list designs as rows with Title, Filename, Format, Dimensions, Stitches, Colors and Smallest Brother Hoop. Click a column header to sort. On smaller windows, use the horizontal scrollbar at the bottom of the table to scroll left and right to see every column. Tick a row's checkbox to select it.

Click **▦ Thumbnail Grid** to go back to picture cards.

## Stitch Preview Background
Above the large preview on the right, the **Stitch Preview Background** list changes the fabric color behind the design: Dark Slate, Warm Linen, Pure White or Midnight. Pick the one closest to your fabric to see how thread colors will look. It only changes the preview; your file is not changed.

## Theme
The **Theme** list in the top bar switches between **Craft Studio Dark** and **Warm Atelier Light**.
