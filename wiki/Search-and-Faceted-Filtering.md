# Search & Faceted Filtering

## Search box
Type any word: a design name, a folder name, a thread color, a tag or a note. Results update as you type.

## Smart Search
The **Smart Search** toggle in the top bar turns on meaning-based search. With Smart Search ON, "winter" also finds snowflake and snowman designs and typos like "wintar" still work ("Did you mean: winter?"). Turn it OFF for exact keyword matching only. Smart Search runs entirely on your computer.

## Faceted Filters (left sidebar)
- **Brother Hoop**: show only designs that fit a hoop size, such as 100x100mm (4x4 in).
- **Format**: show only one file format, such as `.vp3` or `.pes`.
- **Craft Tag**: show designs with one of your tags.
- **Max Stitches / Max Colors**: hide designs above a stitch or color count.
- **Stitch-Out Files Only (Hide .EMB)**: hide Wilcom/Hatch `.emb` working files so only machine-ready files are listed.
- **Reset Filters** clears every filter and the search box.
