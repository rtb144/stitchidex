# Quick Start

1. Unzip the portable `.zip` anywhere (for example `Documents\Stitchidex`) and double-click `stichidexdotnet.exe`.
2. Click **+ Add Folder** in the left sidebar and pick the folder where your designs live (for example your Dropbox embroidery folder).
3. Stitchidex scans the folder. Watch the progress bar in the status row under the top bar; click **Cancel Scan** (or press `Esc`) at any time.
4. Browse your designs in **Thumbnail Grid** or **Table View**. Type in **Search** to find designs by name, folder, color or tag.
5. Tick the **checkbox** on each design you want (or use `Ctrl`-click and `Shift`-click), then click **Tag Selected (N)** or **Send to USB**.
6. Double-click a design (or click **Open in Default App**) to open it in your embroidery software, such as Wilcom Hatch.
7. Click **? Help & User Guide** in the top bar at any time. All help works offline.

Stitchidex never changes, moves or deletes your pattern files. Watched folders are read-only.
